<h1 align="center">
Skyblock Slayers
</h1>


<h2>Mod Description/Purpose</h2>

<p align="center">Skyblock Slayers aims to bring the slayer bosses from Hypixel Skyblock, a persistent MMO-type
minigame found on the Hypixel minecraft server (mc.hypixel.net). The mod in it's finished state will add 4 of the 6 slayer
bosses each with as many difficulty tiers found within Hypixel Skyblock. The mod will also have one
more slayer that is not actually in Hypixel Skyblock, being a slayer boss centred around the Skeleton. This will
take replacement of what is the Sven boss AKA wolf slayer, as for wolves aren't really much of a "hostile mob"
within the context of base Minecraft.</p>

<h2>Slayer Boss Attacks</h2>
<details>
<summary>Revenant Horror</summary>
For damage values listed, they refer to respective boss tiers; T1/T2/T3/T4

_Fields marked with an asterisk indicate temporary information taken from the Hypixel Skyblock Wiki that
are to be changed more accurately to how the boss actually performs in the mod_

- Boss Tiers 1 - 4
  - Melee attack (T1-4)
      - Regular melee attack that the boss performs if you get too close that does 1.5/2.5/8.0/15.0 damage
  - Life Drain (T1-4)
    - Every 3 seconds, drains 1.5/2.5/8.0/15.0 damage from the player
  - Pestilence (T2-4)
    - not yet implemented
  - Enrage (T3-4)
    - not yet implemented
- Atoned Horror
  - Melee Attack
    - Regular melee attack that the boss performs if you get too close that does TBD damage
  - Redemption
    - Heals Rapidly and is immune to magic damage*
  - Explosive Assault
    - Throws explosive TNT*
  - Thermonuclear
    - Charges up and releases a massive explosion*
- "Secret" Unlisted Attacks
  - Yank (T1-5)
    - After 5/10 seconds of the boss not being able to perform a hit on the player, the boss yanks the player toward itself, dealing 1.5/2.5/8.0/15.0/TBD damage
  - Teleport (T1-5)
    - After 15 seconds of the boss not hitting the player, it will teleport itself to the player
</details>
